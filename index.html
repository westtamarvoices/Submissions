    Before you send
    Check every fact is true and matches what you know firsthand.
    For planning applications, stick to planning matters. Council cannot consider things like who the applicant is or property values.
    Talk about the proposal, not people. Leave out criticism of named individuals.
    For question time, ask no more than three questions, and make each one ask a single thing. Leave out follow ons like "and if so" or "if not".
    Lodge before the closing time. Email counts once council's receipt arrives.
    Change the wording so it sounds like you. Personal letters carry more weight than copies.
